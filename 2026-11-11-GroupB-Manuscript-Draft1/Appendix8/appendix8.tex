Table \ref{tab:csv_structure} illustrates the simulated formatting of the comma-separated values (CSV) log utilized by the system across a standard 35-day Pechay grow-out cycle. The \textbf{Smooth GPR} column is populated during offline post-harvest processing rather than by the daily cron routine.
